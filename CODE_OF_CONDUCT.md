# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Keep discussion about the code.

## Expected behaviour

- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change without it being a judgement of
  you or your idea.
- Respect that people contribute in their spare time and across time zones.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption of
discussions, and publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through GitHub's report feature on this repository,
or by contacting the maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and
may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge harmful to
the project.
